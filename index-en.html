The three most expensive mistakes

Each one has already cost companies real money. The audit exists so you don't have to be next.

Mistake · 1

Cultural errors

A brand name that sounds ridiculous in Portuguese, a slogan that offends local culture, product features that don't match how Brazilians actually use them.

One redo costs far more than an audit

Mistake · 2

Regulatory surprises

The goods reach the port before you find out you need INMETRO certification or ANATEL approval, or that the import duty was miscalculated. Cargo held, storage fees still running.

Held cargo plus storage: the loss multiplies

Mistake · 3

Wrong pricing

Priced on Chinese costs and crushed by local competitors, or priced too high, with zero sales on Mercado Livre and the ad budget burned.

A few months of wrong pricing burns far more than ad spend
